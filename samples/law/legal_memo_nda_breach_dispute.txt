
INTERNAL LEGAL MEMORANDUM

TO:      General Counsel, Solace Robotics Inc.
FROM:    Litigation Associate, Fictional & Byrne LLP
DATE:    August 14, 2025
RE:      Potential Breach of Non-Disclosure Agreement — Vendor: Ferrow Dynamics

I. QUESTION PRESENTED
Does Ferrow Dynamics' disclosure of Solace Robotics' proprietary gripper-control
firmware specifications to a third-party integrator constitute a breach of the
Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("NDA") executed on November 1, 2024?

II. BRIEF ANSWER
Likely yes. The NDA's confidentiality clause (Section 3) broadly defines
"Confidential Information" to include technical specifications, and the carve-out
for "independently developed" information (Section 3.4) does not appear to apply,
since Ferrow's internal emails reference direct use of Solace's shared documents.

III. FACTS
Solace Robotics disclosed firmware control specifications to Ferrow Dynamics under
the NDA to evaluate a potential joint integration project. The project did not
proceed. In July 2025, Solace discovered that Ferrow had shared substantially
similar specifications with Quantis Automation, a competitor, while pitching a
similar integration service. Internal Ferrow emails obtained through a mutual
contact reference "Solace's control-loop diagrams" as a basis for the Quantis
proposal.

IV. ANALYSIS
A. Scope of Confidential Information (NDA § 3.1–3.2)
The NDA defines Confidential Information to include "any technical, business, or
financial information disclosed in written, oral, or electronic form, including
but not limited to specifications, schematics, and source code." The firmware
control specifications fall squarely within this definition.

B. Independent Development Exception (NDA § 3.4)
Ferrow may argue the specifications shared with Quantis were independently
developed. However, the referenced internal emails undercut this defense by
explicitly citing Solace's diagrams as a working reference, which is strong
evidence against independent development.

C. Term and Survival (NDA § 6)
The NDA's confidentiality obligations survive termination of the underlying
business relationship for a period of 5 years from the disclosure date (through
November 2029). The alleged disclosure in July 2025 falls within this window.

D. Remedies (NDA § 8)
Section 8.2 provides for injunctive relief "without the necessity of posting
bond," in addition to monetary damages. Section 8.3 includes a prevailing-party
attorneys' fees provision.

V. RECOMMENDATION
We recommend sending a cease-and-desist letter to Ferrow Dynamics within 10
business days, followed by a formal demand for an accounting of all disclosures
to Quantis Automation. If Ferrow does not provide a satisfactory response within
15 days, we recommend proceeding with a motion for preliminary injunction given
the ongoing competitive harm.
