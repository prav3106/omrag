
RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 3, 2025,
by and between Meridian Apartments LLC, a fictitious property management company
("Landlord"), and Rohan Kapadia ("Tenant"), for the rental of the property located at
Unit 4B, 118 Larkspur Lane, Rivermill, State of Cascadia ("Premises").

ARTICLE 1 — TERM
1.1 The lease term begins on April 1, 2025 and ends on March 31, 2026 (the "Initial Term").
1.2 If neither party gives written notice of termination at least 60 days before the end
    of the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews on a month-to-month basis.

ARTICLE 2 — RENT
2.1 Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,850.00, due on the 1st day of each month.
2.2 A late fee of $75.00 applies to any payment received after the 5th day of the month.
2.3 Rent shall be paid by bank transfer to the account designated by Landlord in writing.

ARTICLE 3 — SECURITY DEPOSIT
3.1 Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $1,850.00 prior to occupancy.
3.2 The deposit shall be returned within 21 days of move-out, less any deductions for
    damage beyond normal wear and tear, as itemized in a written statement.

ARTICLE 4 — MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
4.1 Landlord shall maintain the Premises in habitable condition, including plumbing,
    heating, and electrical systems.
4.2 Tenant shall promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs. Landlord shall respond
    to non-emergency repair requests within 5 business days and emergency requests
    (no heat, no water, gas leak) within 24 hours.
4.3 Tenant shall be responsible for damage caused by Tenant's negligence or misuse.

ARTICLE 5 — PETS
5.1 One domestic cat or dog under 40 lbs is permitted with a non-refundable pet fee
    of $300.00 and an additional pet deposit of $200.00.
5.2 No more than two pets are permitted without prior written consent of Landlord.

ARTICLE 6 — TERMINATION
6.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon material breach that
    remains uncured 15 days after written notice.
6.2 Early termination by Tenant without cause requires 60 days' written notice and
    payment of an early termination fee equal to one month's rent.

ARTICLE 7 — GOVERNING LAW
7.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Cascadia, without
    regard to conflict-of-law principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date first
written above.
